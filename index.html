About Us

North Woods Coffee Company is a locally owned café serving small-batch roasted coffee, espresso drinks, Maine-inspired specialty beverages, pastries, and light refreshments.

The café was created as a comfortable neighborhood gathering place where students, professionals, families, travelers, and local residents can enjoy carefully prepared coffee in a relaxed environment. The company purchases coffee from responsible suppliers and roasts in small batches to maintain quality and consistency.

Our Story

North Woods Coffee Company began with a simple idea: excellent coffee should feel approachable. The café combines carefully roasted beans, familiar Maine flavors, and a comfortable neighborhood atmosphere.

Locally Roasted Coffee

Coffee is roasted in small batches to improve freshness and allow the roasting team to maintain consistent flavor.

Community Involvement

North Woods Coffee Company supports local school activities, nonprofit fundraising events, artists, and community gatherings whenever possible.

Sustainability

The company works to reduce waste by using recyclable or compostable service products where practical, encouraging reusable cups, and purchasing coffee through responsible suppliers.
